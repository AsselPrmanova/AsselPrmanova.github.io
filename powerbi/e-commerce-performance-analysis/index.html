Business Performance Analysis Project

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard for an e-commerce / retail business experiencing declining sales and reduced profitability. The objective of the project was to identify root causes of performance issues and provide data-driven recommendations to improve revenue, operational efficiency, and margins.

Key Analysis Areas

  • Sales trends and revenue performance
  • Profitability analysis by product and category
  • Customer behavior and segmentation
  • Regional performance comparison
  • Operational efficiency insights
